HOW IT WORKS

A simple and fast way to find assistance

We connect you with a person who is suitable for your needs.

1️⃣

Describe your needs

Tell us what type of service you need, the location, the date and any important information about your request.

2️⃣

We look for a suitable person

We search our network for a person who matches the characteristics and requirements of your request.

3️⃣

You receive a proposal

We provide you with the necessary information regarding the matching process and the conditions of the service.

4️⃣

The service is carried out

After confirmation, the matched person provides the service at the agreed date and location.

Our goal: to save you time by making it easier to find a person suitable for your everyday needs.

A solution for clients and service providers

AllRund-Service acts as a matching platform. We facilitate contact between people looking for assistance and available service providers.
