Would you like to offer your services?

Join our network and be connected with clients in Hannover and the surrounding area.

📝

1. Present your profile

Tell us about your skills, areas of activity, availability and the services you would like to offer.

🤝

2. Receive opportunities

When your profile matches a request, we may offer you the opportunity to connect with a client.

The details of the service, including the date, location and conditions, are agreed between the parties.

Reliable and professional cooperation can help you gradually develop your network and opportunities.

Why join our network?

Flexibility: choose opportunities that match your availability and skills.

Matching: we facilitate contact with people looking for services in your field.

Network: gradually expand your professional network through new opportunities.

Matching depends, among other things, on client requirements, your profile, your availability and any professional qualifications required for the activity concerned.
