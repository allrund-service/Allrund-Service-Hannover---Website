AllRund-Service Hannover

A platform that makes it easier to connect people for everyday services.

Our goal

AllRund-Service aims to simplify the search for a suitable person for the everyday needs of private individuals and businesses.

We facilitate connections between people looking for assistance and available service providers in Hannover and the surrounding area.

Our vision: to make finding everyday assistance simpler, faster and more accessible.

A matching platform

AllRund-Service acts as a matching platform. We facilitate contact between clients and service providers according to the needs expressed by the client.
